Primary Key:
A primary key uniquely identifies a row in a table.

Foreign Key:
A foreign key creates a relationship between tables by referring to a key in another table.

JOIN:
A JOIN combines rows from multiple tables using a related column.

Normalization:
Normalization organizes relational database tables to reduce redundancy and improve data integrity.

ACID stands for:
- Atomicity
- Consistency

- Durability
